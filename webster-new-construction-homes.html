Webster TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

Webster sits at the heart of the Houston Bay Area, right at the intersection of I-45 and NASA Parkway, and offers something distinct from the larger master-planned communities covered elsewhere in this series: a smaller, more centrally located footprint within the same well-regarded Clear Creek ISD that serves League City. If proximity to the Johnson Space Center employment corridor, Clear Lake, and quick access to both I-45 and Galveston Bay matters to you, Webster deserves a close look.

As of March 2026, the overall median sale price for properties in Webster was $491,746 - worth noting that figure reflects the broader Webster housing market, not new construction specifically, so treat it as area context rather than a new-construction price point.

Active New Construction Communities

  • Midline - A master-planned community developed by Brookfield Residential in partnership with CDC Houston, featuring new homes, trails, parks, and Clear Creek ISD schools. As of this writing, new homes were coming Spring 2026, priced from $450,000.
  • Edgewater - A Highland Homes community also zoned to Clear Creek ISD, offering another entry point into the Webster market.

Webster's new-construction footprint is smaller and more concentrated than the larger corridors in this guide - contact me for current phase and lot availability before visiting.

Schools: Clear Creek ISD

Webster is served by Clear Creek Independent School District, frequently recognized for its strong STEM programs and college readiness outcomes. As always, confirm the exact zoned campuses for any specific lot before signing - I verify this for every client before we move to contract.

Builders Active in Webster

  • Highland Homes - Building in Webster with pricing starting in the $410s at Edgewater, offering one of the more accessible entry points in the Clear Creek ISD footprint.
  • Brookfield Residential - A leading North American new home builder developing Midline in partnership with CDC Houston, with homes priced from $450,000.

Webster has a smaller, more concentrated builder lineup than a large master-planned corridor - ask me for the current quick-move-in inventory at both communities before visiting.

What to Expect on Price

New construction in Webster generally starts in the $410s at Edgewater and $450,000 at Midline. The broader Webster housing market carried a median sale price of $491,746 as of March 2026 - reflecting the area's Bay Area location and Clear Creek ISD zoning, though again that figure covers the whole market rather than new construction specifically. As with any community, confirm the property tax rate and any MUD or PID assessment directly with the builder before comparing.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

I accompany you through every builder visit in Webster, or brief you in advance if you'd rather see a community on your own first. Builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget, so there's no cost to you. That includes a pre-visit briefing on current programs, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing.

Because Webster's new-construction footprint is smaller than a large master-planned corridor, I keep close tabs on quick move-in inventory at both active communities - in a smaller market like this, timing on a specific lot matters more than it does somewhere with dozens of open sections.

Is Webster Right for You?

Webster is a strong fit for buyers who want Clear Creek ISD schools with a smaller, more centrally located footprint close to the Johnson Space Center corridor and Clear Lake, and who don't need the scale of amenities that a large master-planned community offers. If you want more community options and a wider price range within the same school district, League City - just south, and zoned to the same Clear Creek ISD - is worth comparing directly.
