# SEO Audit (Round 2) — allan939.github.io/newconsguide

Date: 2026-04-06

## Quick findings
1. **Content depth was limited to one sales page** — weak for long-tail keywords.
2. **No blog cluster for topical authority** around Houston new construction subtopics.
3. **Limited internal links for informational intent** (mostly conversion CTAs).
4. **Sitemap needed informational URLs** to support discovery/indexing of deeper pages.

## Improvements implemented in this update
- Added a **Blog section** on the homepage with crawlable links to three keyword-targeted guides.
- Added three SEO-focused blog pages:
  - `blog-houston-new-construction-2026.html`
  - `blog-katy-vs-cypress-new-construction.html`
  - `blog-builder-incentives-houston.html`
- Added metadata and canonical tags on each blog page.
- Updated `sitemap.xml` with new blog URLs.

## Suggested next SEO steps
1. Add **FAQ schema** and **Article schema** on blog pages.
2. Publish at least 2 new posts monthly targeting high-intent keywords (location + builder + price band).
3. Add internal links between blog posts and key conversion sections (`#contact`, `#search`).
4. Set up Google Search Console and submit sitemap after deployment.
