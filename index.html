Houston new construction FAQ

The questions buyers ask most often.

Do I need my own agent when buying new construction in Houston?
Yes. The person at the model home works for the builder. My job is to represent you — review the contract, make sure you understand the numbers, and help you use the incentives that are available. And it doesn’t cost you extra. The builder already has that fee built in.
What builder incentives are available right now in Houston?
Rate buydowns (sometimes into the 4–5% range), closing cost credits, and price reductions on certain homes. It changes by community and by the week. I keep a current list for the areas I’m working and can tell you what’s actually available for your budget.
How long does the process take?
Move-in ready homes can close in 30–45 days. Homes still under construction are usually 3–6 months. If you’re starting from a lot, plan on 6–10+ months depending on the builder and weather. We’ll map a realistic timeline on the first call.
Can you help if I only speak Spanish?
Yes. Se habla español. We can do the whole process in Spanish if that’s more comfortable.
