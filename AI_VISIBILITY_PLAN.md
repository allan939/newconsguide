## What actually moves AI citation (not just crawl access)

AI answer engines mostly cite from the same pool of well-linked, authoritative pages that regular search ranks. Priority, in order of leverage:

1. **Backlinks from real Houston sites** — local news, neighborhood blogs, builder partner pages, HAR content.
2. **Third-party "best of" roundups** — being *named* in an existing list is worth more than almost anything on your own site.
3. **Community mentions (Reddit, Nextdoor)** — AI models weight Reddit heavily for recommendation-style queries.
4. **Consistent NAP/citations** — same name, brokerage, phone, and description everywhere you're listed.
5. **Narrow, FAQ-shaped content** — you already have this; keep extending it.
