League City TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

League City, in the Bay Area corridor south of Houston along I-45, pairs Clear Creek ISD - widely regarded as one of the stronger public school districts in the Houston Bay Area - with genuine new-construction variety, from entry-level product to $1M+ homes in the corridor's newest flagship community. It's also one of the closest new-construction submarkets to Galveston Bay and the coast, which shapes both the lifestyle and the builder lineup here.

League City has seen a wave of new community launches recently, which means there's more genuinely new inventory here right now than in some of the more built-out corridors covered in this series.

Active New Construction Communities

  • Legacy by Hillwood - League City's newest master-planned community, spanning 805 acres. New construction here is priced from the $400s to $1M+, across 10 builders: Beazer, Coventry, David Weekley, Drees, Highland, Partners in Building, Perry, Shea, Village Builders, and Westin. First residents moved in during early 2026. The amenity plan includes parks, trails, green spaces, and roughly 60 acres of waterways. With 10 builders in one community, this is one of the widest single-community builder selections in this entire guide series.
  • Westwood - A master-planned community in southern League City with active builders including CastleRock Communities, Lennar, Coventry Homes, and K. Hovnanian, offering new construction from the mid-$400s with resort-style amenities.
  • Lago Mar - Built around what's described as the largest crystal-clear lagoon in Texas, with Tricoast Homes as an active builder. This is a distinctive lifestyle-driven community rather than a pure value play - worth a look if the lagoon amenity itself is a draw for your family.
  • Westland Ranch - Another active League City community; ask me for current builder and pricing details since specifics here shift regularly.

Legacy by Hillwood in particular is brand-new with residents having just moved in during early 2026 - contact me for current phase and builder availability before visiting.

Schools: Clear Creek ISD

Clear Creek Independent School District (CCISD) serves League City and is widely regarded as one of the stronger public school districts in the Houston Bay Area. As always, confirm the exact zoned campuses for any specific lot before signing, since assignment can vary by subdivision - I verify this for every client before we move to contract.

Builders Active in League City

Between Legacy by Hillwood, Westwood, and Lago Mar, League City has one of the deepest builder rosters in this guide series:

  • David Weekley Homes, Highland Homes, Perry Homes, Drees Homes - All active at Legacy by Hillwood, spanning the community's $400s-to-$1M+ range with distinct design and customization approaches.
  • Beazer, Coventry Homes, Partners in Building, Shea Homes, Village Builders, Westin Homes - Also part of Legacy by Hillwood's 10-builder lineup, giving buyers unusually broad choice within a single community.
  • CastleRock Communities, Lennar, K. Hovnanian Homes - Active at Westwood, generally at a more accessible price point starting in the mid-$400s.
  • Tricoast Homes - Building at Lago Mar around the community's signature lagoon amenity.

With 10 builders in Legacy by Hillwood alone, narrowing this list to what fits your budget and design priorities is where I add the most value before you start touring model homes.

What to Expect on Price

League City spans a wide range: Westwood starts in the mid-$400s, while Legacy by Hillwood runs from the $400s up past $1M depending on builder and homesite. That range reflects the corridor's position as an established, higher-demand Bay Area submarket rather than an entry-level growth corridor - expect pricing closer to Katy or The Woodlands than to Baytown or Crosby.

As always, confirm the property tax rate and any MUD or PID assessment directly with the builder before comparing communities.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

I accompany you through every builder visit in League City, or brief you in advance if you'd rather see a community on your own first. Builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget, so there's no cost to you. That includes a pre-visit briefing on current programs, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing.

Given how new Legacy by Hillwood is, I'm tracking phase releases and builder incentive programs there closely - new communities often run their strongest introductory programs in the first several phases, which is worth knowing before you commit to a lot.

Is League City Right for You?

League City is a strong fit for buyers who want Clear Creek ISD schools, Bay Area proximity, and genuine builder variety spanning entry-level to luxury within a short drive of each other. If a smaller, more focused Clear Creek ISD community with a lower price floor is a better fit, Webster - just up the road and zoned to the same district - is worth comparing directly.
