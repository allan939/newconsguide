1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at allanvrealestate.com/ operated by Allan Vega, a licensed Texas real estate agent with AEA Realty / The Ask Ozzie Team, based in Houston, Texas.

By using this website or submitting any form on it, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or submit your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information:

Information you provide directly - When you fill out the contact form or the quick lead bar, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Area of interest (Houston submarket)
  • Price range / budget
  • Any message you voluntarily include

Information collected automatically - When you visit the site, analytics and advertising tools may collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL (how you arrived at the site)
  • General geographic location (city/region level, not precise)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Interactions with page elements (clicks on CTAs, form events)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide you with information about Houston new construction homes, builder programs, and incentives
  • Send you the personalized new construction guide you requested
  • Follow up by phone or email as requested
  • Understand which pages and content are most useful to visitors (analytics)
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising (Meta and Google)

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or significant effects on you.

We will never sell your personal information to any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) - Collects anonymized website usage data to help us understand traffic and visitor behavior. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel - Tracks visits and conversion events (such as form submissions) to help us measure ad performance on Facebook and Instagram. Managed via Meta's Privacy Policy. You can manage ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Google Apps Script (Form Backend) - Form submissions are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint, which stores submissions in a Google Sheet accessible only to Allan Vega. Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • YLOPO Property Search Widget - The property search tool on this site is powered by YLOPO. Using that widget may subject you to YLOPO's own terms and privacy practices.
  • Google Calendar - The scheduling link ("Schedule a free 15-min call") opens Google Calendar. Booking an appointment is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts - Fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • Analytics cookies - Set by Google Analytics to distinguish unique visitors and sessions
  • Advertising cookies - Set by the Meta Pixel to track conversions and enable retargeting on Facebook and Instagram
  • Session storage - Used by this site itself (not third parties) to remember if you've dismissed the sticky form or exit popup during your current browser session. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Doing so may affect the functionality of some parts of the site. You can also use browser extensions such as uBlock Origin or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent tracking.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers - Google (Analytics, Apps Script, Fonts, Calendar), Meta (Pixel), and YLOPO as described above, solely to operate this website and respond to your inquiry
  • Legal requirements - If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Allan Vega or others
  • Business transfer - In the unlikely event of a business reorganization, your information would transfer under the same privacy protections described here

7. Data Retention

Form submission data (name, email, phone, area, budget) is stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to Allan Vega. We retain this data for as long as it is useful for business purposes - typically no more than 3 years after last contact. You may request deletion at any time (see Your Rights below).

Analytics data is retained per Google's default settings (up to 14 months for GA4 user data).

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access - Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction - Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion - Request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing - Reply STOP to any text or email to opt out immediately
  • California residents (CCPA) - You have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11 below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
