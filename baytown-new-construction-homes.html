Baytown TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

Baytown, on the eastern edge of the Houston metro along I-10 and the Houston Ship Channel, has become one of the more active affordable new-construction markets in the region. As of February 2026, the median sale price for newly built homes in Baytown was $314,370, based on 196 newly constructed homes from 13 active builders - a genuinely competitive number relative to most of the Houston metro, and it comes with real builder variety rather than one or two options.

Baytown's economy is anchored by the Ship Channel's industrial and petrochemical employment base, which gives the area a distinct job-market character compared to the retail- and office-driven suburbs west of Houston. For buyers working in that sector, or simply looking for value with a shorter commute to the east side of the metro, Baytown is worth serious consideration.

Active New Construction Communities

  • Bay Creek - A Smith Douglas Homes community with pricing starting from the low $200s, close to shopping, dining, parks, and Goose Creek CISD schools. As of this writing, Bay Creek has a VIP interest list forming ahead of move-ins expected in Fall 2026 - worth getting on that list early if the price point fits.
  • Baytown Crossings - Located near I-10 and Garth Road, featuring homes from Lennar and Beazer Homes. Children here attend Goose Creek CISD schools, and the community's Ashbel Cove section has a planned on-site elementary school - a meaningful convenience factor for families with young kids.
  • Scott's Bend - A newer master-planned community with the Classic Collection offering new single-family homes; ready homes were expected as early as September 2026 as of this writing. Ask me for current status since this is one of the newer entries to the Baytown market.

New sections and builders open in Baytown regularly given the pace of activity here - contact me for the current list before visiting.

Schools: Goose Creek CISD

Baytown is served by Goose Creek Consolidated Independent School District. As with any district, confirm the exact zoned elementary, middle, and high school for a specific lot before signing - new communities like Baytown Crossings' Ashbel Cove section are adding on-site campuses, which is worth factoring into your timeline if a planned school hasn't opened yet when you close.

Builders Active in Baytown

  • Smith Douglas Homes - The builder behind Bay Creek, offering some of the most accessible entry pricing in this guide series at low $200s.
  • Lennar - Building in Baytown Crossings with its "Everything's Included" pricing model, which bundles upgrades into the base price for easier comparison.
  • Beazer Homes - Also active in Baytown Crossings, rounding out that community's builder lineup.
  • Colina Homes, CastleRock Communities, Pulte Homes - Among the roughly 13 builders active across the broader Baytown new-construction market as of February 2026, each with distinct price points and product.

With 13 builders active in a market this size, there's real room to compare - tell me your budget and priorities and I'll narrow the list.

What to Expect on Price

The median sale price for new construction in Baytown was $314,370 as of February 2026, with Bay Creek's entry point starting in the low $200s. That spread makes Baytown one of the more accessible new-construction markets covered in this guide series, with genuine builder variety across that range rather than a single dominant option.

As always, confirm the property tax rate and any MUD or PID assessment directly with the builder before comparing communities.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

I accompany you through every builder visit in Baytown, or brief you in advance if you'd rather see a community on your own first. Builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget, so there's no cost to you. That includes a pre-visit briefing on current programs, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing.

Given how many builders are active here, I track which communities and phases have real current availability versus which are running low on inventory, so we're not chasing sold-out sections.

Is Baytown Right for You?

Baytown is a strong fit for buyers prioritizing affordability, a shorter commute to the east side of the Houston metro, or proximity to Ship Channel-area employment - and who are comfortable with a market whose retail and amenity base is less built-out than the western suburbs. If you want similar affordability with a different geography, Crosby and New Caney & Porter are worth comparing directly.
