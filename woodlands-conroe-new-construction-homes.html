The Woodlands & Conroe TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

The original Woodlands is, for practical purposes, built out. Its last remaining new-construction pocket is the Creekside Park area, and even that is limited. What this means for buyers today: if you want the Woodlands lifestyle - walkable villages, mature tree canopy, extensive trail systems, strong amenities - in an actual new construction home, the real opportunity has shifted north and east into Conroe, along with pockets of Spring, Magnolia, and Willis.

That shift is not a downgrade. Conroe ISD serves both the original Woodlands and this newer growth ring, so families aren't trading school quality for newer inventory - they're trading a built-out address for more house, larger lots, and a lower price per square foot, while staying inside the same school system and a short drive from the Woodlands' employment and retail base along I-45.

Active New Construction Communities

  • Grand Central Park - A 2,000-acre master-planned community straddling the area between The Woodlands and Conroe, right along I-45 and S. Loop 336, about five miles north of The Woodlands proper. It's built around a walkable, urban-feeling town-center concept with nearby shopping, dining, and entertainment. David Weekley Homes is building on 55-foot homesites here, with pricing from the $300s into the millions across the community's full builder lineup - making it one of the widest price ranges of any community in this guide.
  • Creekside Park (The Woodlands) - The newest section of the original Woodlands and effectively the last new-construction pocket inside Woodlands proper. Inventory here is limited and commands a real premium for the address itself - ask me for current availability before assuming there's an open phase.
  • Surrounding Conroe / Spring / Magnolia / Willis communities - Beyond Grand Central Park, this is where the bulk of current new-construction activity in the broader Woodlands market is happening. Because this stretches across several municipalities and school district boundary lines, I'd rather walk you through current options directly than list them generically here - a lot fifteen minutes apart can land in a different school zone or tax structure.

This corridor covers a wide geographic area with more builder and community turnover than a single master-planned community - contact me for a current list of what's actually selling before you start visiting sales offices.

Schools: Conroe ISD

Conroe ISD serves The Woodlands as well as most of this surrounding growth corridor, including Grand Central Park. As with any large, fast-growing district, campus assignment can vary by subdivision and phase, and boundaries do get redrawn as new schools open to keep pace with growth. Always confirm the exact zoned campuses for a specific lot before signing - I verify this for every client before we move to contract, since a boundary shift mid-build is one of the more common surprises buyers run into in fast-growing districts like this one.

Builders Active in the Area

This corridor has one of the deepest builder benches in the Houston new-construction market:

  • David Weekley Homes - The primary builder at Grand Central Park's 55-foot homesite sections, known for a design-forward product and strong customer service reputation.
  • Toll Brothers - Active in the luxury tier across this corridor, offering larger lots and extensive design center customization.
  • Pulte Homes, M/I Homes, Taylor Morrison, Shea Homes, Coventry Homes, Trendmaker Homes, Darling Homes - All active somewhere in the broader Woodlands/Conroe new-construction market, each with a distinct price tier and product style. Given the number of builders here, I typically narrow this list to 3-4 that actually match a buyer's budget and priorities before we visit anything in person.

With this many builders active across a wide area, a pre-visit call to me is especially worth it here - I can tell you which builders are running the strongest current programs before you spend a Saturday driving the corridor.

What to Expect on Price

This is one of the widest price ranges of any corridor covered on this site. Grand Central Park alone spans from the $300s to the millions depending on builder and homesite. Broadly speaking, expect entry-level product in the outer Conroe/Willis/Magnolia sections, mid-range product concentrated around Grand Central Park's production-builder sections, and luxury product in Creekside Park and Grand Central Park's premium homesites. Because the range is so wide, a general price expectation isn't as useful here as it is in a single-community corridor - tell me your target budget and I'll narrow the list to communities that actually fit it.

As always, request the full tax rate and any MUD/PID disclosure directly from the builder before comparing communities - this varies meaningfully across a corridor this large.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

Given how spread out this corridor is, I typically start with a phone or video call to narrow down which of the many active communities actually fit your budget, commute, and school priorities before we visit anything in person. From there, the process is the same as anywhere else I work: a pre-visit briefing on current programs, accompanying you to the builder sales office, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing - all at no cost to you, since builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget.

Is The Woodlands / Conroe Corridor Right for You?

This corridor is a strong fit for buyers who want the Woodlands lifestyle and Conroe ISD schools but need more house, a larger lot, or a lower price per square foot than the original Woodlands can offer. It's also a good fit for buyers who want genuine builder and price-point variety in one corridor rather than a single master-planned community's offering. If your priority is a more established, single-community feel with a longer resale track record, Katy is worth comparing.
