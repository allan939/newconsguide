Katy vs Cypress: Which Area Fits Your New Build Goals?

Published April 6, 2026 · By Allan Vega

Both Katy and Cypress are top choices for Houston-area buyers, but they serve different priorities depending on commute, schools, and budget flexibility.

Katy strengths

Master-planned depth, broad builder selection, and strong buyer demand. Excellent fit for buyers prioritizing community amenities and school reputation.

Cypress strengths

Often better value-per-square-foot in comparable tiers, plus rapid growth in newer communities with active incentive programs.

How to decide

Start with commute tolerance and monthly payment ceiling. Then match builder inventory and incentives to your timeline and must-have features.
