Sugar Land & Missouri City TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

Sugar Land and Missouri City, along the US-59/Fort Bend Tollway corridor southwest of Houston, are among the most established and highest-demand submarkets in Greater Houston. This is a mature, largely built-out area compared to the newer growth corridors further out - which means new construction here tends to concentrate in a smaller number of large, amenity-rich master-planned communities rather than being spread across dozens of new subdivisions.

Buyers who prioritize a top-rated school district, strong long-term resale history, and proximity to established retail, dining, and employment centers (including the Sugar Land Town Square area and the broader Fort Bend business corridor) consistently gravitate here. The trade-off is that entry points tend to run higher than in newer-growth corridors like Baytown or Crosby, and fewer raw lots remain, so timing and community selection matter more than in a market with abundant open inventory.

Active New Construction Communities

Here's where new construction is actually happening in the Sugar Land / Missouri City area right now:

  • Sienna - A 10,800-acre master-planned community in Missouri City and one of the top-selling master-planned communities in the Houston area, with a mix of floor plans, price points, and homesite options from builders including Highland Homes and Shea Homes. Sienna's scale means there's real variety here - from starter price points to move-up product - within one community's amenity package.
  • Lexington Village - A newer-selling community in Missouri City with D.R. Horton as the primary builder, offering a more accessible entry point than the established Sugar Land core.
  • Parkway Commons - Located in Missouri City with direct access to the Fort Bend Tollway, this community sits within Fort Bend ISD and is positioned as a commuter-friendly option for buyers working toward the Galleria or downtown.
  • Telfair & First Colony - These are established, largely built-out Sugar Land communities. New construction opportunities here are limited to occasional infill lots or builder close-out inventory rather than an active, open sales phase - worth asking me for current availability rather than assuming a live sales office.

Sugar Land and Missouri City move fast and have less standing inventory than newer-growth corridors - contact me for current phase and lot availability before making a trip out.

Fort Bend ISD

Fort Bend ISD is one of the most diverse and highly regarded school districts in Texas, recognized for strong academic programs and a well-developed extracurricular tradition. It's one of the primary reasons buyers choose this corridor over comparably priced options closer to central Houston.

As with any district this size, campus assignment varies by subdivision and even by phase within some communities. Always confirm the exact zoned campuses for a specific lot before signing - I verify this for every client before we move to contract.

Builders Active in the Area

  • Highland Homes - Building in Missouri City with pricing starting in the $590s, Highland positions toward the move-up end of this market with a design-forward product.
  • Toll Brothers - Active in the area's luxury tier, offering larger homesites and extensive design center customization for buyers who want a semi-custom feel without a full custom build.
  • Shea Homes - Building within Sienna, Shea brings a broader price range to that community alongside its other builder partners.
  • D.R. Horton - The primary builder at Lexington Village, offering the most accessible price point currently available in the Missouri City new-construction market.

Builder lineups and phase availability shift regularly in this corridor - call me before visiting so I can brief you on what's actually open for sale.

What to Expect on Price

This is one of the higher-priced new-construction corridors in the Houston metro. Highland Homes' Missouri City product starts in the $590s, and Sugar Land / Missouri City generally carries some of the highest median new-construction prices in the region given the maturity of the area and the strength of Fort Bend ISD. Sienna's scale does create some variation - ask me for current pricing by builder and phase, since a community this large isn't a single price point.

As with any master-planned community, request the full property tax rate breakdown (including any MUD or PID assessments) directly from the builder before comparing communities - effective tax rates vary meaningfully by subdivision in Fort Bend County.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

I accompany you through every builder visit in this corridor, or brief you in advance if you'd rather see a community on your own first. Builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget, so there's no cost to you. That includes a pre-visit briefing on current programs, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing.

Because standing inventory is tighter here than in newer-growth corridors, timing and relationship with the sales office matter more. I track which communities in this corridor have real remaining lot availability versus which are effectively sold out, so we're not chasing phases that are already gone.

Is Sugar Land / Missouri City Right for You?

This corridor is an excellent fit for buyers who want a mature, established community with a long resale track record, top-tier Fort Bend ISD schools, and easy access to Sugar Land's employment and retail base - and who can absorb a higher entry price for that certainty. If you want a similar school-district-first profile with more open inventory and a lower price floor, Katy is worth comparing directly.
