Crosby TX New Construction Homes: 2026 Buyer's Guide

August 8, 2026  ·  By Allan Vega

Crosby, northeast of Houston along US-90 near Lake Houston, is a different kind of new-construction market than most others covered in this series. Rather than one or two flagship master-planned communities, Crosby's new construction is spread across a larger number of smaller builder sections and subdivisions - as of February 2026, 21 different builders were active here, more than any other corridor in this guide.

That structure has real implications for how you shop this market. Instead of picking a single community and comparing floor plans within it, buying in Crosby usually means comparing builders and specific subdivisions against each other directly - which is exactly the kind of legwork a buyer's agent who tracks this market closely can save you.

What's Building in Crosby Right Now

As of February 2026, Crosby's new-construction market included 470 newly built homes from 21 respected builders, at a median sale price of $281,390. That builder count is genuinely unusual for a market this size, and it means availability and pricing shift subdivision by subdivision rather than following one community's phase release schedule. I don't want to hand you a static list of subdivisions here that could be stale by the time you read this - Crosby's builder-lot landscape turns over faster than a single master-planned community would. Call or message me for the current active list matched to your budget and preferred lot size.

Schools: Crosby ISD

Crosby is served by Crosby Independent School District. As always, confirm the exact zoned campuses for any specific lot before signing - I verify this for every client before we move to contract.

Builders Active in Crosby

The leading developers building new construction in Crosby include:

  • Lennar - The "Everything's Included" model bundles upgrades into the base price, simplifying comparison across floor plans.
  • CastleRock Communities - Known for competitive standard inclusions relative to price point.
  • Centex - A long-established, value-focused builder brand with a track record in entry-level product.
  • Ashton Woods - Positions slightly above entry-level with more design flexibility.
  • K. Hovnanian Homes - A national builder with a broad price range depending on subdivision and floor plan.
  • M/I Homes, Coventry Homes, Century Communities - Also active in the Crosby market, rounding out the field of 21 builders as of February 2026.

With this many builders active in one market, comparing them side by side is where a buyer's agent earns their keep - call me before visiting any Crosby sales office.

What to Expect on Price

The median sale price for new construction in Crosby was $281,390 as of February 2026 - one of the more accessible price points covered in this guide series. Given the number of builders active here, that median masks real variation by subdivision and floor plan; tell me your target budget and lot preferences and I'll narrow the current list to what actually fits.

As always, confirm the property tax rate and any applicable district assessments directly with the builder before comparing subdivisions.

How the Buyer's Agent Process Works Here

Because Crosby's new-construction market is spread across so many builders and subdivisions rather than concentrated in one or two master-planned communities, I typically start by narrowing the field to two or three subdivisions that actually match your budget and priorities before we visit anything in person. From there, the process is the same as anywhere else: a pre-visit briefing on current programs, accompanying you to the builder sales office, a contract review before you sign, coordination of a third-party pre-drywall inspection, and a documented final walkthrough before closing - all at no cost to you, since builders pay my commission out of their marketing budget.

Is Crosby Right for You?

Crosby is a strong fit for buyers prioritizing affordability and genuine builder choice, and who don't need the single-community amenity package that a large master-planned development like Sienna or Cross Creek Ranch offers. If you want a similarly affordable price point with a more concentrated, single-community structure, Baytown and New Caney & Porter are worth comparing directly.
